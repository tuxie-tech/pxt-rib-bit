## Enum `Button`

Identifies one of the six Rib:bit buttons. The numbers match the button's light position on the NeoPixel strip.

- `L1 = 0`: Left button 1.
- `L2 = 1`: Left button 2.
- `L3 = 2`: Left button 3.
- `R3 = 3`: Right button 3.
- `R2 = 4`: Right button 2.
- `R1 = 5`: Right button 1.
